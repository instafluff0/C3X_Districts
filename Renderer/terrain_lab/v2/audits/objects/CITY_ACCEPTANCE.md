# Q7 city acceptance — provisional, not promoted

The mixed and neighboring held-out registered terrain views are the primary
composition witnesses. The held-out town is wholly concealed by forest/jungle
in `out/Q1/Q7-presentation/registered-mixed-holdout-v2/h12-z1-pan00.bmp`.
Its unchanged anchor and full source building/roof bounds are published in
`metadata/registered-mixed-holdout-v2/corridors.json`. This is a failed composed
visibility gate, not a reason to move the city or overpaint vegetation. Q0/Q4
own vegetation placement integration. The newer linear witnesses are assessed
separately in `Q7_PRESENTATION_AUDIT.md`.

## Implemented composition

All 20 effective culture/era pools and 44 unique normalized city bodies are
loaded through every worked-state geometry/material binding. The historical
runtime builder selected the first mesh/material, which omitted modern glass
facades. Complete source parts now stay together, including roofs and facades.
No source vertices, UVs, textures, or proportions were repainted or stretched.

Stable deterministic recipes use 4/7/11 bodies. Town is a prefix of city, and
city is a prefix of metropolis: source identity, uniform scale, yaw and position
remain unchanged. Tall bodies enter first; candidate slots reserve full source
XY bounds and penalize projected bounding-box overlap. Spacing, number of
components, pool scale, and growth are separate controls. The `golden` row is
a reconstructed golden-angle comparator using complete source bodies, not a
byte-identical frozen L17 render.

The modern component-ID control (`modern-id-02/view.png`) shows distinct full
bodies and more purposeful gaps in the stable lower row. Ground collisions and
projected overlap are reported separately in `METRICS.json`; bounding rectangles
are conservative and are not claimed as exact silhouette visibility.

One approved all-pool gallery (`all-pools-01`) covered all 60 pool/size cells at
noon/normal zoom. Two actual-pixel crop sheets were directly inspected. Long low
European and Middle Eastern ancient compounds initially shrank whole pools to
tiny roofs. The focused `ancient-earth-02` iteration adds a capped uniform pool
scale floor; the new source cottages are more legible. Low source architecture
still needs contextual/night/two-zoom review. The all-pool gallery is inventory
evidence, not the primary beauty target.

## Day, night, walls and routes

`modern-check` and `modern-emissive-only` cover noon, sunset, midnight, sunrise
at both zooms with deterministic repeats. The source window emission is localized;
unlit architecture stays blue/dim at midnight. Emission is not claimed to cast
light onto neighbors. Source Light/VFX sockets remain unresolved; no invented
lamp lights were added. Source walls and gates use repeated uniformly scaled
mesh pieces. Their visual enclosure was inspected, but all style/capital/wall
states and route entry geometry are not accepted.

The through-route fixture reserves an explicit diagnostic corridor before
choosing buildings and uses source gates at its two entries. Q5's full-footprint
query rejects intersections for all 20 metropolis pools and preserves growth
prefixes. This synthetic envelope is not a rendered real road/rail/river.
Exact Q3/Q5 real source corridor adoption, wall/gate intersections, numeric
overlays and composed route readability remain required. Source geometry bounds
include roof overhangs; cast shadows across routes are a separate phenomenon.

## Source and material limits

`metadata/*/source-instances.json` publishes source mesh/material/channel hashes,
exact uniform preprojection matrices, UV/normal identity and runtime bindings.
Q1 independently validated 96 explicit matrices and all referenced mesh/material/
texture hashes (`audits/sampling/q7-sidecar-validation.json`), plus the all-pool
world triangles without using Q7 emitter functions. This validates source
transform preservation, not full shading fidelity.

Base color (t124) and emission (t116) are bound. Authored vertex normals are yaw
rotated. AO, normal_0, normal_1 and gloss are source channels but remain unbound;
tangents are absent from normalized city vertices. Secondary-normal/gloss engine
meaning is not established by channel names. Transfers retain the normalized
declarations. Do not guess BC4 semantics or add brightness to hide absent channels.
Current registered city materials declare opaque. Family alpha/owner colors and
socket attachment transforms remain open.

Source +X is provisionally mapped to front; yaw 0/+90 projects southeast/southwest.
The mathematical quadrant test passes, but ambiguous/symmetric source fronts
still need per-asset art verification. No claim that all entrances are approved.

## Coverage that still prevents city acceptance

- Held-out city/vegetation composition, exact final route/bank envelopes and
  neighboring city density with retained labels/HUD.
- Full source material/channel, capital, owner-color and socket states.
- Shared mesh-derived body-to-body/body-to-ground shadows in the new final
  composition, including authored alpha. Legacy local-shadow views are diagnostic.
- All effective pool difficult cases at four phases/two zooms and scrolling.
- Q8 combined acceptance, Windows parity and the 192-tile promotion gate.

The coordinator deferred the broad matrix during storage pressure. None of
these gates is waived or silently marked passed. Historical L17/L21 handoffs
and accepted v1 evidence remain unchanged.
