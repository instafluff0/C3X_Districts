# Renderer Lab (historical `terrain_lab` path)

Renderer Lab is the standalone visual-development harness for all map graphics.
It began as a terrain material-truth tool, so the directory and executable retain
their `terrain_lab` names. It bypasses the production/game-integration renderer;
its baseline renders four connected grassland tiles directly from normalized
material files, while later gates expand through terrain, map objects, units,
lighting, animation, and effects.

The current executable remains lab-only. `PLAN.md` continues the numbered lab
ladder with dunes, marshes, volcanoes, rivers, roads, railroads, resources,
civilization/era/size-specific cities, mines, farms/irrigation, animated units,
and their combined scene. L9's accepted historical gate uses 48 contiguous
tiles; L10 and L11 preserve their accepted 96-tile evidence. From L12 onward
every gate uses at least 192 contiguous Civ III tiles containing the current and
all preceding accepted layers. Those cells are selected as a 16x12
true-adjacency diamond from an authoritative installed Windows BIQ containing
the current system; `test.biq` remains preferred when it qualifies. The next
lab gate waits for explicit user visual approval;
each approved system may be handed independently to Game Integration. These
future gates are plans, not claims that the present harness or integration path
supports those categories.

The source investigation and active integration for L10 are recorded in `DUNE_SOURCE_AUDIT.md` and `L10_DUNE_AUDIT.md`. They confirm that the installed Civ VI base source uses a procedural `DuneDesertHills` layer plus height-bearing sand decals rather than a conventional authored dune mesh.

Run `RUN.bat`. It rebuilds the single executable, hot-compiles `terrain_lab.hlsl`, and writes ignored outputs to `Renderer/preview/out/terrain_lab/`:

- `grass_albedo.bmp`: the source swatch on the left and an unlit four-tile patch on the right. Both use the same base-color texture and color pipeline. This is the color, filtering, and UV-scale baseline.
- `grass_material.bmp`: the same source swatch alongside the patch with only micro-normal and restrained specular response enabled.
- `grass_relief.bmp`: the material pass on one densely subdivided, continuous 2x2 surface with broad procedural landforms and geometric normals. This deliberately generic relief proves shape scale and lighting before any authored hill asset is admitted.
- `grass_relief_shadow.bmp`: the same geometry with directional self-shadow and broad valley/contact occlusion derived from the macro heightfield. Compare it directly with `grass_relief.bmp`; no material or geometry input changes between the two.
- `grass_authored_hill_standard.bmp`: the raw `standard` Civ VI hill heightfield on the left and one continuous world-space crop driving the shadowed grass patch on the right. The heightfield is range-normalized but otherwise unshaped; it is never restarted per tile.
- `grass_authored_mountain_standard_01.bmp`: three source-truth panels—height, blend footprint, and false-colored region IDs—beside variant 01 geometry. The geometry uses the explicit blend field as an experimental footprint multiplier over the complete 2x2 patch; region IDs are displayed but not yet assigned speculative material meanings.
- `coast_straight_beach.bmp`: beach and cliff material swatches beside a gently curved shared coastline, with a sloped authored beach strip and flat diagnostic water.
- `coast_corner_cliff.bmp`: a frozen L6/L7 diagnostic retained for comparison but no longer generated by `RUN.bat`; the active art direction omits cliffs.
- `terrain_beauty_l9_3.bmp`: the production-bound portion of the L9 scene—rolling grass, one authored mountain, a broad clean beach, layered water using normalized multi-scale lean maps, and authored crash-foam surf without diagnostic panels. Vegetation is intentionally absent until the installed Civ VI forest/jungle package is normalized; no proxy geometry is rendered.
- `terrain_beauty_l9_3_no_relief.bmp` and `terrain_beauty_l9_3_no_water.bmp`: isolation renders for the accepted L9 terrain and water layers. The no-water pass intentionally retains the submerged bed so the water contribution can be judged directly.

- `terrain_beauty_l11_marsh.bmp`: the native 2560x1440 L11 candidate. It dynamically selects exactly 96 visible source tiles from the authoritative GOG `test.biq` as a 12x8 true-adjacency diamond and carries a non-rendered two-cell adjacency halo so filtered fields remain faithful at the crop boundary. It preserves exact feature ownership, blends compatible ground materials and relief across shared edges, and adds the normalized `GrassMarsh` material plus source-authored `CLUTTER_MARSH` projected decals. The current revision distributes all five normalized standard-mountain variants across BIQ mountain cells and calibrates their authored shoulders, skirts, height, and snow to the Civ III 2:1 footprint. Grassland hills use a macro-filtered, world-continuous sample of the actual standard hill field, remapped so its authored contours produce broad irregular bodies instead of elevated ownership diamonds; their skirts may cross compatible neighboring land and taper at water or mountain boundaries. Corrected surface coordinates match across both BIQ adjacency axes. A filtered viewport-wide ownership scalar, cell-center ownership anchors, and source-coordinate contour variation define the visible coast. Ground, bed, and water are emitted as ordered viewport-wide passes; bed and water are clipped against that one contour instead of being restricted to water-owned diamonds. A separate continuous optical-depth field removes coast/sea plates, and shared source-coordinate material weights break up straight land-family edges without either incident tile disagreeing. The Beach material blends into actual grassland, plains, desert, and marsh, while Shallows, ocean bed, water density/scatter, multi-scale normals/LEAN, gloss/masks, and crash/ripple/turbulence foam supply the source-backed water stack. Hills and authored relief flatten through the continuous shoreline-distance field before meeting the beach. The lab intentionally has no gameplay features or production-renderer constants. Coast and water are isolated lab implementations rather than production code. The completed L8 vegetation adapter supplies verified forest and jungle bodies plus generic ArtDef-derived placement records; L13 and later promotion previews require the alternate `Civ5EnvironmentSkin` terrain and `Civ5EnvironmentVegetation` body packs. L9.3 and L9.4 remain frozen comparison baselines; L9.5 and its 48-tile promotion scene, the 96-tile L10 dune gate, the 96-tile L11 marsh gate, and the 192-tile L12 volcano and L13 river gates are accepted. L13A is a pending visual candidate and applies its restrained frame tone map only in the eight lighting modes. Edit the five numeric `terrain_lab.exe` arguments in `RUN.bat` to change material UV scale, normal strength, exposure, relief height, and authored-hill UV scale. Editing the HLSL does not require changing production renderer code.

Run `python3 Renderer/tools/asset_compiler/clutter_blp_extractor.py` from the repository root to rebuild the ignored local vegetation pack, its source-evidence report, and the source-independent `vegetation_runtime.bin` consumed by L9.4. Preview any normalized feature without reading Civ VI formats at runtime with, for example, `python3 Renderer/preview/render_feature_asset.py --pack Renderer/packs/VegetationNormalized/manifest.json --asset feature/jungle/palm_01 --output Renderer/preview/out/clutter/jungle_palm_01_contact.png`. `preview/render_feature_set.py` uses Pillow only as a lab compositor to generate the labeled L8 acceptance sheet; the mesh and DDS rendering path remains source-agnostic.

The first gate is simple: `grass_albedo.bmp` must look like one coherent material sampled across a connected patch, with no per-tile restart or invented lumpiness. The relief gate is equally narrow: `grass_relief.bmp` must read as a few broad, connected landforms whose lighting follows their geometry, without introducing tile-frequency bumps. Authored hill assets remain excluded until both gates pass.

`PLAN.md` records the complete lab ladder and the exit gate for returning the proven result to the production renderer.
