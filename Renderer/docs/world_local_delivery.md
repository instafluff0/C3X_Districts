# Persistent world inputs and local updates

This delivery separates persistent compiler inputs from native view observations and publishes local changes into the existing world owner. The immediate comparison revision is `0469689fc8f0398e8fd9a22669b7834918cee4f0`. Earlier builds of this step are intermediate evidence; final measurements and qualification are recorded below.

## Ownership and invalidation

`CapturedScene` owns copied world records and immutable canonical `WorldSnapshot` leases. Canonical inputs have world coordinates and zero camera anchors; native `ObservationView` retains the actual current occurrence and anchors. `compilation_view(true)` pins the immutable world lease, while legacy compilers borrow the native observation view for their existing foreground read lifetime. Camera-only captures reuse the world snapshot. Local publications copy changed 8×8 input blocks and share unchanged blocks; old worker leases remain valid and charged until released.

`WorldPreparationSources` shares that snapshot and a strong scope/revision-matched coast topology owner. It no longer copies the entire current observation map for each camera. Resident content still requires appearance, semantic, coast, world, river, native-anchor and handle-generation proofs. Missing inputs and evicted generations cannot authorize reuse.

`ScenePublication` coalesces accepted changes independently of camera tickets. Actual world-input changes report changed tiles; the existing preparation schedule invalidates only regions whose 8-tile core and 12-tile input halo depend on those tiles. Camera movement reprioritizes unfinished regions while preserving completed work. Map/viewer/configuration changes retire the world scope; asset, device and native detail/extent changes retain their existing conservative preparation barriers.

## Initial capture and native authority

The first certified native tile traversal seeds the map/viewer generation synchronously in bounded 128-record pages before requesting the first camera. Each page copies game-thread facts into the renderer publication path. Initial representative unit-body copies follow that seed. Admission or bootstrap failure prevents a falsely complete first custom frame. Normal redraws in an already completed scope remain asynchronous. Explicit reconciliation remains a recovery path for changes whose closure cannot be certified locally. A native culture-level transition can fill or release territory beyond the city radius; it starts bounded world reconciliation and requests one viewport recapture when the current viewer has a completed display, even if the city itself is offscreen or its body is unchanged. No-op culture/yield recalculations retain the cheap comparison path.

`CITY_BODY_KNOWN` permits the current native city recipe on explored tiles, including explicit city absence. `NATIVE_OVERLAYS_KNOWN` permits viewer-aware roads, railroads, route style, irrigation edges and the native irrigation/mine/pollution/crater/hut/camp fields. Those native methods retain their remembered-overlay rules under fog; camp admission preserves the native present-camp and viewer checks.

Partial facts merge into the existing record and immutable world inputs without granting full-art authority, `PREFETCH` or draw eligibility. Unmarked hidden halos preserve admitted city/overlay facts. Marked captures can replace or remove their own fields, while unrelated resource, raw tile-building/effect and unit policies remain separate. A missing full-art record still closes whole-world region preparation. Actual normalized recipe changes revise appearance, invalidate compiled variants and enter the local dependency closure. Population and label-only changes do not alter geometry identity; native current observations retain their presentation fields.

## Current presentation and limits

Onscreen explored ocean, river and coastal-wave material motion uses the current copied cosmetic clock under fog. Prefetch-only, offscreen and never-explored records do not start that clock. Fog eligibility remains independent of resident geometry and material age. Environment lighting uses the current frame/environment inputs rather than a remembered city or overlay recipe. Hidden units, their shadows and their reflected contributions remain subject to current visibility.

Fresh maps report actual animation eligibility. Their retained callback skips GPU asset/render work and map imports for unchanged static or invisible views. The projected callback holds the completed image; the canonical callback reports no change. Later visible unit additions, changes, removals, motion, pending resource adoption, content/visibility changes and zoom still progress through the same live sampler. Hidden/offscreen unit events do not invalidate its completed visible-pose proof.

This is an ownership and update-delivery change. It does not eliminate first-use terrain/object compilation, uploads, residency pressure or recovery after eviction, and does not implement a new material page format or qualify retained zoom. Source-level reuse and local closure are not evidence of general FPS, GPU execution time or physical display cadence. Navigation-only captures cannot qualify mutation, reveal/hide or turn-driven local changes.

## Measurements

Final candidate measurements use the real 1498 AD disposable save at 2240×1260 with normal water, waves and reflections. Quiet phase windows match the immediate `0469689f` receipts. They are sampled successful presentations, not GPU execution time, physical scanout or isolated component cost; early navigation windows include startup/recovery work.

| Quiet phase | Immediate baseline presentations/s | Candidate presentations/s | Change |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Fixed scroll | 6.805 | 5.447 | −20.0% |
| Idle after scroll | 14.430 | 16.814 | +16.5% |
| Zoom and rapid reversal | 8.582 | 4.674 | −45.5% |
| Settled zoom | 18.663 | 18.334 | −1.8% |

These results show navigation regressions and an idle improvement, rather than a general performance gain. Quiet helper private peaks were 3,785,375,744 bytes for scroll and 3,701,784,576 for zoom (baseline 3,809,988,608 and 3,701,227,520). First authoritative maps arrived at 31.842 and 30.433 seconds in those quiet runs; the first sampled presentations were later. Whole-world initial seed copied 8,450 records in 67 pages in 36.956 ms of CPU/API time (capture 7.516, query wait 3.529, admission wait 25.811). Initial unit bootstrap copied 77 bodies for 76 representatives; the first fresh unit snapshot contained 76.

Initial geometry volume remains 1,511 scheduled, 1,501 consumed and ten rejected/recovered, with 787,743,168 GPU-buffer bytes. Worker CPU spans and upload request spans do not measure GPU elapsed time. In the profiled six-destination run, the first cold north and south destinations did not reach a full matching handoff before the next 20-second input. The other authoritative recovery spans were 4.162, 1.098, 7.413 and 0.891 seconds; sampled matching screen arrivals were 4.537, 1.536, 8.566 and 1.540 seconds. The two incomplete destinations are retained as such. The intermediate candidate receipts also remain available; their measurements are not substituted for the final candidate. No queue-drain claim is made for game termination; the standalone ordered consumer separately drained.
