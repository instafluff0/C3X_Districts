Renderer Lab · Cities · candidate pack v5

Making Civ VI cities read like Civ III cities

Production cities sat darker than the grass around them, covered under half of their footprint and stood on rivers and bridges. The candidate keeps every selected Civ VI building and texture and changes placement, ground and lighting response so each city reads as one bright block per tile.

Drag the slider in any frame: left is the current production pack, right is the candidate. All frames are standalone Lab renders through the production D3D11 renderer at noon, not live-game captures.

Placement

  • Buildings grow about their own anchors until they nearly meet; Industrial and Modern grow first and largest.
  • Gaps are refilled with more of the same city's buildings.
  • Industrial cities gain smokestacks, a factory and a power plant; Modern cities gain towers, a water tower and a plant.

Ground plate

  • A crisp plate under the buildings and lanes: packed earth, cobble, grey cobble or concrete by era.
  • It fades out over rivers, water and mountains.

Site awareness

  • Outskirt buildings yield to rivers (bridges stay visible), water, mountains and steep slopes.
  • Forest closes in where a building yielded.
  • The palace, walls and civic centre always stay.

Light response

  • Brighter, higher-contrast bodies with a highlight shoulder, so plaster and glass keep detail.
  • Night window light is unchanged.
